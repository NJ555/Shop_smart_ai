# Warranty Policy — v2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01. Region: GLOBAL.

## Smartphone Warranty
Smartphones carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering battery and screen defects.
Physical damage and water damage are not covered.

## Laptop Warranty
Laptops carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty, extendable to 2 years for PREMIUM members.

## Appliances Warranty
Large appliances carry a 2-year warranty including compressor coverage for ACs and washers.

## Claim Process
Raise a support ticket with order ID and issue photos. Support verifies purchase and warranty window, then approves a simulated replacement or repair.
