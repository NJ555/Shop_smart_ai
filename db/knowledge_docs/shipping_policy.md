# Shipping Policy — v2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01. Region: GLOBAL.

## Delivery Estimates
Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. PRIME members get free shipping above $35 and faster simulated delivery (2-3 days). PREMIUM members get priority simulated dispatch.

## Tracking
Every order creates a simulated shipment with tracking number SIM-TRACK-xxx.
Shipment states: PENDING, LABEL_CREATED, IN_TRANSIT, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERED, DELAYED.
Customers can track from Order Detail with a live timeline.

## Delays
If a shipment is DELAYED, customers may contact support or request cancellation only if the order has not shipped yet.
Once shipped, cancellation is blocked with reason ORDER_NOT_ELIGIBLE_FOR_CANCELLATION.

## Shipping Fees
BASIC members pay a simulated $9.99 fee below $50. PRIME and PREMIUM get free shipping above $35.
