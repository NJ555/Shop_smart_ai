# Laptop & General Return Policy — v2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01. Region: GLOBAL.

## Standard Returns
Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full simulated refund.

## Opened Products
Opened laptops can be returned within 7 days if physical damage is not present.
The laptop must include all original accessories, charger, and box.
If physical damage or liquid damage is found, the return is rejected and a support ticket is opened instead.

## Non-returnable Items
Gift cards and clearance final-sale items cannot be returned.

## How To Return
Raise a return from Order History, pack the item, and hand it to the simulated carrier.
Refunds are simulated and complete within 5-7 business days after pickup.
